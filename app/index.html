How it works

The board analyzes, drafts, and challenges. The OCM lead decides and delivers.

This application runs the operating model from the playbook: ten specialist advisor personas and a Board Chair, governed by one Operating Charter and one Client Change Context Brief.

Context Brief

One shared scope, timeline, and set of assumptions. Every advisor reads the same brief, so their advice stays coherent.

Operating Charter

Outcome focus, facts separated from assumptions, no fabricated data, client-specific advice, and a standard response format.

Ten advisors

Each built from the same eight elements, with a distinct challenge style, so you get useful debate rather than ten generic consultants.

Four operating modes

  1. Solo advisor: one expert drafts a deliverable.
  2. Cross-functional: two to four advisors tackle a shared problem; the Chair synthesizes.
  3. Board challenge: advisors independently give the strongest case for and against, and the evidence that would resolve it.
  4. End-to-end: the Chair guides discovery through sustained adoption.

Limits and responsible use

Advisors never invent facts, scores, baselines, or leader commitments; they use [placeholders] and label assumptions. Everything is a draft until you validate it. Use fictional or anonymized briefs unless your firm and client policies allow otherwise. Your brief and session are saved only in this browser.
