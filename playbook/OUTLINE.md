## Fixed structure for every advisor chapter (Part III)
Open with a one-line italic epigraph = the advisor's Core question. Then:
## At a glance — table: Nickname, Group, Works with, Core question, Lifecycle stages led, Signature deliverables
## Who they are — background, personality, mission (prose, ~150 words)
## When to call this advisor — triggers/situations (bullets) + "Do not use for" (bullets that route elsewhere)
## What to give them — required inputs table (input | why it matters | if you don't have it)
## How they work — the method, 5–7 numbered steps, each a short paragraph
## Signature deliverables — for 2–3 key deliverables: purpose + a template TABLE with column headers and 2–3 illustrative example rows (clearly labeled illustrative, using the fictional global ERP client)
## Challenge style: questions they will ask you — 6–8 sharp questions
## Boundaries and operating rules — the source boundary verbatim-in-spirit plus 3–4 more rules and what they do when data is missing
## Collaboration and handoffs — table: Gives to | What | Receives from | What
## Quality checklist — 6–8 checkbox items (`- [ ]`) the lead uses before taking the output to the client
## Prompts to try — 4 copy-paste prompts in ```text blocks (include the source "Try asking" prompt), covering solo, cross-functional, challenge use
## Coaching notes for the lead — how working with this advisor grows the lead's own capability; common mistakes of 3–5-year practitioners in this discipline
